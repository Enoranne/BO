# BO_Ta_Vie — PISTE 0 Playable Structure

## Status
**DESIGN ONLY.**

This document organises PISTE 0 into a playable narrative structure without adding mechanics to Sprint 5.

## Core rule
The player should remember **moments**, not objectives.

The game may have a narrative spine, but that spine should be experienced through domestic life, sounds and curiosity rather than a quest log.

## Structure model
Use three layers:

### A — Narrative spine
A very small number of beats required to understand Malo's creative evolution.

### B — Memory field
Optional/semi-optional moments that enrich the childhood and the cassette archive.

### C — Ambient life
Unscored, often non-recordable details that make the house feel inhabited.

The player should spend substantial time in B and C without being told that they are "side content".

---

# Phase 0 — Christmas 1982 / Discovery

## Narrative purpose
Malo discovers that a machine can hold a moment and give it back.

## Required spine
- receive / take Fisher Price;
- record Ronan;
- listen back.

## Memory field examples
- Fisher button fascination;
- wrapping paper;
- fireplace;
- family room tone;
- spontaneous laugh.

## Ambient life
Parents exchange looks, fire moves, tree ambience, room creaks, gift handling.

## Exit condition
Not "all sounds collected". Exit when the first-recording beat has landed emotionally and narratively.

---

# Phase 1 — The house becomes audible

## Narrative purpose
Malo starts treating ordinary domestic sounds as worthy of attention.

## Spine
No hard checklist.

The story can progress after Malo has demonstrated curiosity with the recorder, but no specific fridge/gate/wind count is required.

## Memory field candidates
- corridor creak;
- fridge;
- cutlery;
- door/latch;
- Ronan moving elsewhere;
- Mother's off-screen voice;
- bathroom resonance later.

## Design pattern
Let sounds leak through walls and doorways before areas become fully playable.

This gives the house acoustic depth before geometric expansion.

---

# Phase 2 — Garden / Sound hunting

## Narrative purpose
Recording becomes an activity Malo deliberately pursues.

## Signature beats
- gate / BONJOUR;
- wind / Mother's call;
- swing squeak;
- gull;
- moped at the world edge.

## Player freedom
The player chooses where to wait and what to record.

No sound-map icons.

## Soft guidance
- distant sound enters first;
- Malo can move toward it;
- world event may already be underway;
- the player may be early, late or perfectly placed.

## Emotional rule
The best take is not always the cleanest take.

---

# Phase 3 — Malo starts organising

## Narrative purpose
Repeated recording becomes a personal practice.

## Spine
Introduce a small physical review moment in the bedroom/studio.

## Physical language
- CASSETTES
- BIEN
- RATÉS MAIS GARDER
- optional À REFAIRE

## Player action
Listen, name optionally, classify, perhaps decide to try again later.

## Important
Do not unlock this by "collect 10 sounds". Unlock because Malo has reached a narrative point where organisation feels natural.

---

# Phase 4 — Recording becomes storytelling

## Narrative purpose
Malo stops merely collecting reality and begins creating with it.

## Signature beats
- Ronan suspect / mock investigation;
- Radio Malo genesis;
- `Radio Malo est complètement génial`;
- biscuit special edition;
- homemade jingles and announcements.

## Interaction style
Performative rather than menu-driven.

Examples:
- speak into recorder;
- play a previously captured sound at the right moment;
- trigger a simple playback sequence;
- hear family reaction.

## Avoid
Do not introduce a full nonlinear editor just because Malo is "editing" creatively.

---

# Phase 5 — Persistence / Experimentation

## Narrative purpose
Malo's standards and curiosity increase.

## Signature ideas
- horse / persistence;
- tape repair;
- television bricolage;
- repeated attempts;
- first awareness of sound quality and placement.

## Player choice
Retry because the player/Malo wants to, not because a score says the take is 68%.

## Memory principle
Earlier failed takes remain available and can later become more valuable than improved versions.

---

# Phase 6 — MK2 transition

## Narrative purpose
A more capable device changes Malo's creative vocabulary.

## New verbs may later include
- pitch;
- two-track thinking;
- Sound-on-Sound;
- more deliberate editing/juxtaposition.

## Rule
These are device-gated capabilities, not global player powers.

The Fisher era must remain simpler and more tactile.

---

# Phase 7 — Radio Malo

## Narrative purpose
Private sound collecting becomes authorship and broadcast identity.

## Principle
This should feel like the culmination of habits formed earlier:
- noticing;
- recording;
- keeping mistakes;
- naming;
- replaying;
- arranging;
- narrating.

Radio Malo should not appear as a detached minigame.

---

# Discovery density
A zone should usually contain:
- 1 strong recognisable sound landmark;
- 2–4 smaller contextual sounds;
- 1 character association;
- 1 possible recording accident/overlap;
- 1 reason to revisit later.

Do not fill a room with ten glowing interactables.

# Player guidance budget
Before using explicit objective text, ask whether the beat can be communicated through:
1. sound;
2. motion;
3. character behaviour;
4. composition/light;
5. object state;
6. contextual prompt.

Only then use explicit text.

# Missability philosophy

## Repeatable sources
Gate, fridge cycle, swing, recurring household actions can return naturally.

## Semi-rare sources
Wind gust, gull, moped should recur but not on demand.

## One-off human moments
A specific laugh, phrase or accidental voice bleed may be truly missable.

The game should tolerate that. Different players can own slightly different childhood archives.

# Completion philosophy
PISTE 0 is complete when Malo's creative arc is understandable, not when a database is 100% filled.

Do not expose a completion percentage for sounds.

# Sprint boundary
Sprint 5 implementation remains limited to the existing Christmas1982 first-recording loop and its visual/player-feel validation.

Sprint 6 may prove memory/archive and temporal capture on a tiny scale before any broad PISTE 0 content rollout.
