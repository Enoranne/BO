# Christmas1982 — Sonic opportunity map

## Purpose
Tie the architecture of the house directly to BO's core recording mechanic. This is a design map, not a Sprint 5 gameplay expansion.

The principle is simple: every future room should justify itself through **sound**, memory, character or interaction density. A room that adds only floor area is not worth building.

## Salon — canonical first-recording space
Primary role: onboarding and emotional anchor.

Potential sounds:
- Ronan voice / laugh;
- Fisher Price button mechanics;
- cassette motor / transport click;
- fireplace crackle;
- wrapping paper;
- gift box cardboard;
- sofa fabric movement;
- Christmas tree ornaments / branch movement;
- family room tone.

Sprint 5 rule: no new required recording is added here. RonanTest remains the only gameplay capture required for acceptance.

## Corridor
Primary role: sound bleed and anticipation.

Potential sounds:
- floorboard creak;
- door latch;
- distant kitchen dishes;
- muffled parents' voices;
- bathroom plumbing resonance;
- Ronan moving in another room.

Design value: the corridor should make the house feel larger acoustically before it becomes larger mechanically.

## Kitchen
Primary role: dense domestic sound laboratory.

Potential sounds:
- refrigerator compressor / hum;
- fridge door seal;
- cupboard door;
- cutlery;
- plate / glass handling;
- coffee maker;
- kettle;
- tap and sink;
- chair scrape;
- Mother's voice;
- French-door handle toward garden.

Narrative hooks already compatible with PISTE 0:
- fridge / mother comic beat;
- biscuit special edition;
- domestic voices captured accidentally.

## Bathroom
Primary role: resonant acoustic experimentation.

Potential sounds:
- tap;
- bathtub water;
- drain;
- shower-curtain rings;
- pipe knock;
- room echo;
- bidet/tap if retained in final art direction.

Mechanical opportunity later: Malo discovers that the same voice or object sounds different in a reflective room.

## Bedroom zone
Primary role: private creativity and sibling identity.

Potential sounds:
- construction bricks / toys;
- drawers;
- cassette cases;
- record sleeve;
- turntable start/stop if narratively retained;
- bed springs;
- pencil / paper;
- Ronan's music;
- whispered recordings;
- tape repair handling.

Later mechanics:
- CASSETTES / BIEN / RATES MAIS GARDER organisation;
- sound lists;
- first editing experiments;
- future Radio Malo preparation.

## Garden
Primary role: first outdoor recording playground.

Potential sounds:
- wind in tree;
- swing / portique squeak;
- footsteps on grass/gravel;
- improvised cabin wood;
- bird / gull in distance;
- neighbour ambience;
- Mother calling from the house;
- leaves;
- gate carried from the garden boundary.

Known PISTE 0 compatibility:
- wind / "MALO TU FAIS QUOI ?";
- persistence / horse-adjacent observational moments where appropriate;
- outdoor sound-hunting identity.

## Tree / cabin
Primary role: creative refuge.

Potential sounds:
- wood creak;
- nail / improvised construction detail;
- branch movement;
- elevated wind;
- distant street;
- cassette playback in a small wooden enclosure.

## Gate
Primary role: iconic recurring sound landmark.

Potential sounds:
- metal squeak;
- latch;
- wind-induced movement;
- slam;
- hand scraping metal/paint.

This should eventually become one of the most recognisable sonic objects in the game.

## Street edge
Primary role: suggest a wider world without building a city.

Potential sounds:
- moped pass-by;
- neighbour voice;
- distant dog;
- car pass-by;
- footsteps;
- bicycle;
- wind;
- distant children;
- Radio / television leaking from another house.

## Density rule
For a future playable zone to graduate from visual shell to gameplay space, target at least:

- 3 meaningful ambient/recordable sound opportunities;
- 1 character or memory association;
- 1 visually recognisable landmark;
- 1 reason to revisit or listen differently later.

This prevents BO from becoming a walking simulator built from empty rooms.

## Sprint 5.4 constraint
The Cyclops spike should only create architectural context for these future possibilities. It must **not** implement these sound sources yet, except for temporary non-gameplay ambience if needed for presentation testing.
