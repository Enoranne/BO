# Christmas1982 — Sprint 2 spatial layout

This document locks the placeholder spatial composition before production art.

## Camera-facing composition

The camera occupies the open/front side of the room and looks toward the back wall.

- **Fireplace:** back-left, dominant warm practical source.
- **Sofa:** back-center, leaving visual depth behind Malo.
- **Christmas tree:** back-right.
- **Gift pile:** right-center, between Malo and the tree.
- **Coffee table + rug:** central composition and physical obstacle.
- **Malo spawn:** front-center/left.
- **RonanTest:** center-right, reachable after collecting the Fisher Price.
- **Fisher Price:** near the gift area, visually associated with Christmas morning.

The front wall is intentionally invisible for the camera but has an invisible collision boundary so Malo cannot leave the set.

## Visual contract

Sprint 2 remains **placeholder-only**. Geometry and colors are there to establish blocking and visual hierarchy, not final production design.

The target art direction for later replacement remains:

- late-1970s / early-1980s family interior;
- warm tungsten practical lighting;
- amber / burnt-orange dominant palette;
- deep brown shadows;
- restrained saturation;
- cinematic composition rather than free-camera game staging.

## Locked gameplay scope

No new gameplay is introduced by the set dressing. The only accepted loop remains:

`Malo -> Fisher Price -> REC -> RonanTest -> STOP -> PLAY`
