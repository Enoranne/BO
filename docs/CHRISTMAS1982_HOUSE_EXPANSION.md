# Christmas1982 — Compact house expansion

## Purpose
Extend the current salon into a believable small family house without turning Sprint 5 into an open-world project.

The existing salon remains the canonical anchor. Its locked positions for Malo, Fisher Price, Ronan, fireplace, sofa, tree, gift pile and camera are not moved by this document.

## Design principle
The target is a **dense narrative micro-hub**: a compact domestic space where rooms are close enough to remain production-feasible but distinct enough to support sound discovery, memory and future interaction.

## Phase A — immediate Sprint 5 shell

### Salon — canonical room
Already implemented as the gameplay space. Improve architecture and dressing only.

### Corridor / transition
Add a short circulation zone behind or beside the current camera-safe salon volume. It should establish that the salon belongs to a real house rather than a stage box.

Target functions:
- visual depth through an open doorway;
- future route to bedrooms and bathroom;
- sound bleed from the rest of the house;
- no new gameplay objective during Sprint 5.

### Kitchen glimpse
Create a partial kitchen visible through a doorway, not yet fully traversable if that would jeopardize the current blocking.

Visual anchors:
- late-1970s / early-1980s cabinetry;
- warm laminate / wood tones;
- period refrigerator silhouette;
- coffee maker / kettle / dishes as future recordable-sound candidates;
- door or French-door relationship toward the garden reserved for later expansion.

## Phase B — future playable domestic hub

### Kitchen
Future sound opportunities: refrigerator motor, cupboard, cutlery, coffee machine, running water, adult conversation.

### Bathroom
Future sound opportunities: tap, bathtub water, shower curtain rings, ventilation, plumbing resonance.

### Shared / adjacent bedroom zone
Malo and Ronan need a believable relationship to the corridor and salon. The final room-sharing decision can remain flexible until the narrative pass requires it.

Future props:
- toys / construction bricks;
- records or turntable where narratively justified;
- books and school objects;
- cassette storage;
- early creative-work surfaces.

## Phase C — exterior micro-hub

### Garden
Must connect naturally from the house and support a small number of strong sound landmarks rather than broad empty terrain.

Reserved landmarks:
- swing / climbing frame;
- large tree;
- improvised treehouse / cabin;
- gate;
- boundary toward neighbour / street.

### Street edge
Only a short readable slice is needed initially. It exists to support passing sounds and the feeling of a wider world: moped, gate, wind, neighbour, distant voices.

## Production constraints
- No streaming-world architecture.
- No vehicles as controllable gameplay.
- No crowd simulation.
- No NPC navigation requirement in Sprint 5.
- No room may invalidate the canonical first-recording path.
- New architecture must be modular enough to be replaced progressively by authored meshes.

## Suggested build order
1. Preserve current salon dimensions and blocking markers.
2. Add doorway/corridor silhouette.
3. Add non-traversable kitchen glimpse.
4. Validate current camera composition.
5. Only then make corridor/kitchen traversable.
6. Bedrooms/bathroom after the salon visual slice is accepted.
7. Garden and street edge after interior continuity is stable.
